Changes are based on 665863f. Historical TEST, RC and Universal Final archives are unchanged. The earlier Windows packaging repair is included as a separate reproducible packager and preserved READY_TO_INSTALL delivery.

## Three focused changes

1. Candidate economics now appears directly in the Trader status panel. The bounded history shows player/rarity/trade, BIN, observed remaining time, comparable asking value, estimated 5% fee, configured costs, estimated net profit, required minimum, qualification and exact rejection. NOT VALUED identifies disabled valuation, missing limits/evidence, stale responses or preliminary safety rejection. Price eligibility alone never means profitable. The existing View Logs button is no longer needed for these details; this delivery does not claim to repair that separate native button.
2. Existing native search returns older listings first in measured responses. The supported adapter passes criteria and page to the EA service; no supported server newest-first switch was found. Improved pagination first verifies native offset/stride, seeks farther pages within its request budget, validates their actual offsets, and fills skipped pages. Productive/near-fresh scopes get at most one bonus per four planner turns. Offset changes/replays are rejected, trade IDs deduplicated, and no fixed page is assumed fresh. The page ceiling remains a safety budget, not a coverage claim.
3. The original planner had a full-range broad backstop and recursive splits, but its explicit upper-edge probe (previous native price step through the ceiling, e.g. 99500–100000) could retain up to 20 consecutive page turns. Only fresh observations were highlighted. This explains the concentration; 99500 was a scope boundary, not a universal minimum. The improved build seeds overlapping quarter ranges across the configured range, retains broad searches and adaptive splits, and limits consecutive page turns to two. Cheaper bands get early explicit turns; native minimum/other filters remain preserved.

## Limits and precise remaining uncertainty

No fresh-window candidate appeared in these bounded live observations, so this session cannot prove faster live fresh discovery or live positive/negative valuation examples. Both are demonstrated through the actual installed production factories with controlled market responses. Live observations used the improved strategy before the final one-line skipped-page queue correction; that correction was subsequently covered by regression and final packaged clean-install tests. Current comparable asking prices are not completed-sales evidence or guaranteed profit. A3540–3600s remaining duration is a fresh-window observation, not proof of listing creation. All price ranges receive intents, but bounded pagination, server ordering, shifting results, native filters and market timing prevent any exhaustive coverage claim. No live transaction/reconciliation or production readiness claim is made.

Review found a skipped-page queue error after an empty frontier. A failing regression reproduced it; removing visited pages from that queue fixed it. All final regression and extracted-install tests then passed. No purchase guard or permission behavior was weakened.
